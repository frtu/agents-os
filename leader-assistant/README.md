# OMNI

> **Give leaders back significant execution capacity.**

OMNI is an AI-powered execution system designed to help leaders manage many concurrent initiatives without losing time to context switching, micro-decisions, waiting, and follow-ups.

The goal is not to replace the leader's judgment.

**The goal is to remove the work surrounding the judgment — so the leader can spend more time making the decisions that matter.**

---

## 1. The Problem

As leaders become responsible for more initiatives, their work increasingly becomes **wide rather than deep**.



A typical request rarely consists of a single decision:

```text
Request
  ↓
Understand context
  ↓
Investigate / gather information
  ↓
Break down the problem
  ↓
Ask people for input
  ↓
Wait
  ↓
Review responses
  ↓
Make micro-decisions
  ↓
Follow up
  ↓
Review again
  ↓
Resolve
```

Each individual request may be important.

The problem is that **many of these requests happen simultaneously**.

A leader may have:

- An interview between two packed meetings
- A product question requiring investigation
- A migration project requiring analysis
- Multiple people waiting for decisions
- Several conversations that need follow-up
- Previous context that needs to be reconstructed

The result is not simply "too much work".

It is **fragmented decision-making**.

### The real cost

When initiatives overlap, leaders spend significant time:

- Reconstructing context
- Switching between topics
- Deciding what to do next
- Waiting for people or information
- Remembering outstanding actions
- Reviewing partially completed work
- Repeating decisions that could have been prepared in advance

This reduces the amount of time available for the work only the leader can do:

> **Making decisions, applying judgment, and being accountable for the outcome.**

---

# 2. The OMNI Idea

OMNI separates a complex request into **two loops**.

```text
                         ┌─────────────────────────┐
                         │       OMNI REQUEST      │
                         │ Context + Objective     │
                         └────────────┬────────────┘
                                      │
                     ┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
                     │                                 │
                     ▼                                 ▼
          ┌─────────────────────┐          ┌─────────────────────┐
          │      LOOP 1         │          │      LOOP 2         │
          │  Produce the work   │          │ Drive the request   │
          │                     │          │ to resolution       │
          └──────────┬──────────┘          └──────────┬──────────┘
                     │                                 │
                     ▼                                 ▼
             Usable artifacts                  Context / Tracking
             Detailed checklist                Waiting / Reminders
             Structured outputs                 Progress / Reporting
                     │                                 │
                     └────────────────┬────────────────┘
                                      │
                                      ▼
                              Leader decision
```

## Loop 1 — Produce usable work

The first loop converts a complex problem into a sequence of **simple, uninterrupted steps**.

Instead of asking:

> "How do I solve this entire problem?"

OMNI asks:

> "What is the next concrete piece of work that can be produced?"

Each step should create a **usable output**.

Examples:

- Pre-interview preparation
- Interview question bank
- Candidate assessment
- User stories
- Investigation summary
- Decision options
- Analysis
- Meeting preparation
- Cost-analysis draft

The output is not simply an AI answer.

It is a **structured artifact that the leader can review, adjust, and use**.

---

## Loop 2 — Drive the request

The second loop manages the request around those detailed activities.

It captures:

- Initial request
- Context
- Objective
- People involved
- Current status
- Outstanding actions
- Dependencies
- Waiting states
- Decisions required
- Progress
- Final resolution

OMNI can then help:

- Track progress
- Preserve context
- Identify what is blocked
- Remind people or the leader
- Surface what needs attention
- Report progress
- Continue the workflow after waiting
- Bring the request toward completion

The goal is to prevent:

> **"I know I had something about this, but where are we now?"**

---

# 3. The Key Principle

OMNI is not an autonomous replacement for the leader.

There are two things the leader cannot delegate:

### Accountability

The leader remains responsible for the final decision and outcome.

### Taste

The leader decides what "good" looks like, which trade-offs matter, and when the output is good enough.

Therefore:

> **OMNI does not replace you in resolving the request.**
>
> **It removes the micro-decisions and interruptions around the request — and gives you back the decision.**

# 4. Example — Engineering Interview

Interviewing is a simple example of the broader problem.

An interview may appear:

- Between two meetings
- As an unexpected additional round
- The day before an interview
- With a format different from the usual system-design interview

The leader still needs to provide a high-quality and consistent assessment.

## Current workflow

A typical preparation looks like:

```text
Review resume
    ↓
Review previous conversations
    ↓
Understand what was already assessed
    ↓
Think about what to probe
    ↓
Choose priorities
    ↓
Develop questions
    ↓
Decide how deep / wide to go
    ↓
Conduct interview
    ↓
Remember context
    ↓
Write assessment
    ↓
Tune score / recommendation
```

This creates several problems.

### Context switching

If preparation happens one day earlier, the leader needs to reconstruct the candidate context again before the interview.

### Inconsistent depth

Different candidates can receive different levels of probing depending on available preparation time and mental bandwidth.

### Lower-quality responses

When an interview appears with only a few minutes of preparation, the leader has less time to formulate the best questions and follow-up angles.

---

# 5. OMNI Interview Demo

The demo focuses on **Loop 1**.

The starting point can be as simple as:

> "I have an engineering screening in 5 minutes. This is not the usual system-design round."

OMNI already has the relevant context.

It can combine:

- Candidate resume
- Previous interview context
- Previous assessment
- Interview type
- Expected competencies
- Areas already covered
- Areas that still need probing

Then it produces a **pre-interview question bank**.

## Example output

### Interview objective

What we need to establish during this round.

### Priority areas

The most important areas to validate first.

### Deep questions

Questions designed to test depth of understanding.

### Wide questions

Questions designed to explore breadth and experience.

### Follow-up questions

Potential probes based on likely answers.

### Evidence to look for

What a strong, weak, or ambiguous answer might reveal.

### Suggested flow

A practical sequence for the interview given the available time.

The leader does not need to create the interview plan from scratch.

They need to **review, adjust, and apply judgment**.

---

# 6. The Target Experience

The intended experience is:

### Before the interview

**5 minutes**

Review and tune the generated preparation.

```text
Context
   ↓
OMNI
   ↓
Question bank
   ↓
Leader review
   ↓
Interview
```

### After the interview

**~10 minutes**

Use the interview context and notes to produce and refine the assessment.

```text
Interview notes
   ↓
Structured assessment
   ↓
Evidence / signals
   ↓
Score
   ↓
Leader tuning
   ↓
Recommendation
```

The important part is that the **same context continues through the workflow**.

The preparation is not discarded once the interview starts.

It becomes part of the context used to interpret the interview.

The value is not simply:

> "AI can generate interview questions."

That is already possible.

The value is:

> **AI turns a fragmented request into a continuous execution flow.**

The question bank is one artifact in a larger process.

```text
Interview request

      ├── Context

      ├── Previous assessment
      │
      ▼
```

# 8. The Broader Vision

Interviewing is only one use case.

The same mechanism applies whenever a leader receives a request that requires multiple steps, people, decisions, or waiting periods.

Examples:

### Product management

```text
New product question
→ Gather context
→ Identify gaps
→ Analyse
→ Create user stories
→ Review
→ Track implementation
```

### Investigation

```text
Investigation request
→ Gather evidence
→ Break down hypotheses
→ Ask teams
→ Wait for responses
→ Consolidate
→ Recommend action
```

### Cost optimisation

```text
Cost concern
→ Gather data
→ Identify drivers
→ Analyse options
→ Validate assumptions
→ Compare trade-offs
→ Recommend action
```

The common pattern is:

> **A request starts simple, but execution becomes fragmented.**

OMNI aims to keep the request moving without requiring the leader to continuously carry all of the context in their head.

---

# 9. Context Is a First-Class Concept

One important lesson from the initial work is that **AI needs dedicated context**.

An interview should not accidentally inherit irrelevant context from:

- Product management
- Cost optimisation
- Other projects
- Other candidates
- Unrelated conversations

OMNI therefore needs to understand the context of the work being performed.

The goal is not simply to have "more memory".

The goal is:

> **The right context, for the right initiative, at the right moment.**

This makes the system more reliable and reduces context contamination.

---

# 10. Lessons Learned

## 1. Start with the smallest useful artifact

Don't try to automate the entire request first.

Break it down until each step can produce something immediately usable.

> **Small outputs create momentum.**

---

## 2. AI should absorb micro-decisions, not accountability

The leader should not have to decide every tiny execution detail.

But the leader must remain in control of:

- Priorities
- Quality
- Trade-offs
- Final decisions
- Accountability

> **Automate execution. Preserve judgment.**

---

## 3. Context continuity matters

The preparation should become part of the execution.

The questions generated before an interview should help structure the assessment afterward.

Likewise, an investigation should preserve its context while waiting for responses.

> **Don't restart the thinking every time the workflow resumes.**

---

## 4. The hard part is the machinery

Generating content is increasingly easy.

The difficult part is building the machinery around it:

- Context management
- Approval
- Workflow execution
- Waiting
- Streaming UI
- Progress tracking
- Reporting
- Human intervention
- Recovery
- Reliable state management

This machinery is what turns an AI assistant into an **execution system**.
