# 1. 5-Minute Presentation

## Slide 1 — The Problem — ~30 sec

### **Leadership time is fragmented by execution**

Leaders increasingly manage many initiatives in parallel.

Each request creates:

**Context → discussion → waiting → follow-up → micro-decisions**

The requests are important.

But overlapping them creates constant context switching and reduces decision-making efficiency.

**Key message:**

> We don't need AI to make every decision for us.  
> We need AI to remove the work surrounding the decision.

---

## Slide 2 — The Idea — ~30 sec

### **OMNI turns one complex request into two loops**

**Loop 1 — Produce**

Break the problem into small, uninterrupted steps that each produce a usable artifact.

**Loop 2 — Drive**

Capture context and continuously track, remind, report, and move the request toward resolution.

**Leader stays at the decision point.**

---

## Slide 3 — Live Demo: Interview Prep — ~2 min

### **"I have an interview in 5 minutes."**

Show OMNI generating:

1. Candidate/context summary
2. What has already been assessed
3. Priority areas
4. Deep questions
5. Wide questions
6. Follow-up questions
7. Evidence/signals to look for
8. Suggested interview flow

Then highlight:

> **5 minutes to review and tune — instead of reconstructing the interview from scratch.**

---

## Slide 4 — Same Context, Next Step — ~45 sec

### **The artifact doesn't disappear after the interview**

The generated question bank becomes part of the interview context.

After the interview:

```text
Notes
 ↓
Assessment
 ↓
Evidence
 ↓
Score
 ↓
Leader tuning
```

Target:

> **~10 minutes to review and tune the assessment.**

The bigger point:

> **The AI isn't just generating content. It is maintaining execution continuity.**

---

## Slide 5 — The Bigger Loop — ~45 sec

### **From producing work → driving work**

The interview is just one example.

For every initiative, OMNI can eventually manage:

**Context → Progress → Waiting → Action → Reporting → Resolution**

A leader can see:

- What is happening
- What is blocked
- Who is waiting for whom
- What needs a decision
- How far the request has progressed

**The leader doesn't have to carry the workflow in their head.**

---

## Slide 6 — Lessons & Vision — ~30 sec

### **Give leaders back execution capacity**

Three lessons:

**1. Automate the micro-decisions, not accountability.**

**2. Preserve dedicated context throughout the workflow.**

**3. The real value is the machinery around AI — not the generation itself.**

### Final message

> **OMNI doesn't replace the leader in resolving the request.**
>
> **It removes the interruptions and execution overhead around the request — giving the leader back the decision, the judgment, and ultimately significant execution capacity.**

---

# 2. Current Focus

The immediate goal is not to solve every leadership workflow.

It is to prove the pattern with a small number of high-value workflows.

### Phase 1 — Prove Loop 1

Generate high-quality, reusable artifacts from complex requests.

Initial example:

**Interview → preparation → question bank → assessment**

### Phase 2 — Connect Loop 2

Add:

- Persistent context
- Request tracking
- Progress
- Waiting states
- Reminders
- Reporting
- Resolution tracking

### Phase 3 — Scale Across Leader Work

Extend the same machinery to:

- Product management
- Investigations
- Project execution
- Cost optimisation
- Cross-team requests
- Other recurring leadership workflows

---

# 3. Success Criteria

OMNI should ultimately improve three things.

### Time

Reduce time spent preparing, coordinating, following up, and reconstructing context.

### Quality

Increase consistency and depth of execution.

### Capacity

Allow leaders to manage more initiatives without proportional growth in execution overhead.

The ultimate metric is not:

> "How many AI tasks did we automate?"

It is:

> **"How much more decision-making capacity does a leader get back?"**

---

### Shorter version
