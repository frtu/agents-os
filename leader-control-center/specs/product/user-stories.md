# User Stories

## Epic Management

As a leader

I want to organize initiatives into Epics

So that related work remains grouped.

---

## Story Planning

As a leader

I want to define Stories inside an Epic

So that business outcomes become executable.

---

## Task Planning

As a leader

I want to break Stories into Tasks

So that AI can execute smaller units of work.

---

## Manual Execution

As a leader

I want to manually start Tasks

So that I remain in control of execution.

---

## Workflow Monitoring

As a leader

I want to monitor every running execution

So that I know what AI is currently doing.

---

## Human Decision

As a leader

I want AI to stop only when necessary

So that interruptions remain minimal.

---

## Artifact Review

As a leader

I want to inspect outputs

before approving continuation.

---

## Timeline

As a leader

I want to understand everything that happened

without reading entire conversations.

---

## Attention Queue

As a leader

I want every workflow needing attention grouped together

so I never miss an important decision.

---

## Retry

As a leader

I want to retry failed executions

without recreating planning.
