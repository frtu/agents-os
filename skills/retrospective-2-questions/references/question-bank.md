# Retro question bank — by theme

Generic questions the facilitation guide draws from. **Tailor, don't copy**: every question in the guide should name the team's own item ("the CDC crashes after hours", "the quarter-end capacity scramble"), not the generic wording below. Pick 3–5 per theme; the guide numbers them 1…N across all themes.

Question types, in the order a block usually runs: **open** (what happened) → **impact** (how much it cost) → **cause** (why) → **decide** (what we change, who, by when).

---

## 0. Set the stage (opening)

- In one word, how was this cycle for you?
- On a scale of 1–5, how safe does it feel to say what didn't work today? *(if low, anonymise the gathering round)*
- What is the one outcome that would make this hour worth it?

## 1. Carry-over — did last time's commitments work?

- {Action}: done, in progress, dropped — and **did it fix the pain it targeted?**
- {Pain} came back although {action} is done. What did the action miss?
- {Action} never started. Was it capacity, ownership, or the wrong action?
- {Risk} was on our watch list. Did the early signal move?
- We left {decision} open. Who decides, and by when?

## 2. Delivery & flow

- Where did work wait longest this cycle — and why there?
- What did we commit to vs finish? What explains the gap?
- Which piece of work was bigger than we thought when we started it? What would have told us earlier?
- What did we start that we should not have started?

## 3. Quality & reliability

- Which incident or defect cost us most this cycle? What let it through?
- What caught problems early — and what should have and didn't?
- Where are we relying on a person noticing rather than a check?

## 4. Operations, on-call & toil

- What woke someone up or interrupted focus most often? Was it actionable?
- Which requests could users serve themselves if we built one thing?
- Which manual task did we repeat more than three times?

## 5. Priorities & scope

- When two things competed this cycle, how was it decided — and was that the right call?
- Is anything we're doing not traceable to a goal? Should we stop it?
- Which decision are we waiting on from outside the team?

## 6. Collaboration & communication

- Where did a hand-off or dependency slow us down? What would make it smoother?
- What did we learn late that someone knew early?
- Which meeting earned its time, and which didn't?

## 7. Tooling & tech debt

- Which piece of debt taxed us most this cycle — in hours, incidents, or delays?
- What would we fix first if we had one week with no feature work?
- Which tool helped or hurt most?

## 8. People, morale & sustainability

- Is the current pace sustainable for the next cycle? What would make it so?
- Who carried more than their share, and how do we spread it? *(ask about load, not blame)*
- What did you learn, and what do you want to learn next?
- What are we still doing only because one person knows how?

## 9. Wins — what to protect

- What went well because of a choice we made (not luck)? How do we keep it?
- What's the evidence — a number, a before/after?
- Which win is at risk of being dropped next cycle?

## 10. Risks ahead

- What could hurt us next cycle that isn't hurting yet?
- What's the early signal we'd see first? Who watches it?
- Which risk is cheap to reduce now and expensive later?

## 11. Decide — turning insight into actions

- If we could change only three things next cycle, which three?
- For each: who owns it (one name), by when, and how will we know at the next retro that it worked?
- Is the cause known? If not, is the action "investigate by {date}" rather than "fix"?
- What are we explicitly **not** doing, and saying so?

## 12. Close

- Confidence vote (1–5) that these actions will happen.
- What should we change about how we run this retro?
- One appreciation for someone this cycle.
