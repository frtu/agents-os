# Guide — Team load, delegation, ops and AI use

**Load when:** signs of overload or a high-output/low-outcome team; deciding whether to take over toil; delegating a project; cross-team delivery where one party lags; deciding where AI fits. Cases: N, O, P.

## Default moves

1. **The leader owns the overload signal.** Slow down overall, keep high value plus business-as-usual, and park exploration in a backlog that gets revisited.
2. **Two-loop org:** the leader and leads absorb and scope incoming asks; the execution loop gets predictable, time-boxed sprints with buffer.
3. **Route topics explicitly to named leads.**
4. **Depth by priority:** P1 escalations get just enough; recurrences become P2 optimization; owned areas get real thought; P3 stays shallow.
5. **Before taking over toil, walk the real process**, then negotiate conditions (scope, increment control, who keeps incident risk).
6. **Automate up to the last reversible step.** Destructive actions and approvals stay human.
7. **Delegation design:** juniors drive deterministic but unfamiliar work; seniors review the design; consult the seniors first. Pitch: "you're the leader here: pull information, design, they review".
8. **Cross-team lag:** pre-align roles 1:1 or cancel; one topic per meeting; exit gates; vertical slices; owner → their leader → shift the date.
9. **AI use:** augmented dev (code, tests, rollback) is safe; augmented ops needs a human in the loop. Accountability and taste can't be delegated. Don't make reviewers QA generated pages.
10. **"Do ops enough to know how to do dev."** Automate what recurs; don't automate what you haven't done by hand.

## Questions to fire

- "Which items produced an outcome someone noticed?"
- "Who absorbs new asks before they reach the team?"
- "What's the last reversible step?"
- "Whose scope is late, and what did their leader do about it?"

## Traps

- Spreading a late team's scope across everyone.
- 20% of everyone on prevention instead of one person at 50%.
- The leader keeps planting new seeds. Hold back: "no new topics".
- Becoming the new human relay after taking over the toil.

## Morale

Recognize specific contributions ("I connected you and hoped magic would happen; it did"). Shield, then involve. Share upward context with a label ("0.1 version, don't act on it").
