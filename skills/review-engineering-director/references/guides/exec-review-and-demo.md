# Guide — Exec reviews, demos and vision refreshes

**Load when:** preparing or debriefing an exec demo, vision review or risk assessment, or after an exec reacted badly ("wrong direction", "not ambitious"). Cases: E, R. Templates: `../templates/vision-refresh.md`, `../templates/risk-assessment.md`.

## Default moves

1. **Pick the artifact for the audience.** Execs get the end shape and a logical architecture; teams get milestones. Never let an increment stand in for the vision (H15).
2. **Agenda for a live demo:** what we can do → what we can't yet → why (current approach) → what it would take and when (H20). Credit the builder by name in the room.
3. **Demo craft:** show capability, not cosmetics. Remove noise sources (turn off unrelated features). Have a crisp status ready, because execs ask in the corridor.
4. **Log the exec's live edge case as a bug on the spot.** That beats defending it.
5. **Debrief within 24 hours:** "What did you see? How did they take it?" Translate the reaction for the team: which part was right, and what was timing.
6. **Vision hygiene:** refresh on a cadence. Your boss reviews first, then you present together. "Not tomorrow; when it's ready."
7. **Risk assessment ≠ plan.** For "should we go?", deliver risks, business consequences and sampled cases, and let the owners answer.

## Questions to fire

- "Is the exec seeing the end shape or a milestone?"
- "What can't it do yet, and did we say so before he found out?"
- "What is the intent behind his literal technology ask?"
- "What decision does this artifact drive?"

## Traps

- More technical detail after a vision rejection. It deepens the mismatch.
- Promising ambition you can't deliver. Stay delivery-driven, not slide-driven.
- Showing the engine instead of the outcome. Let them drive and feel the acceleration.
- Bringing half-thought work to the top boss. Use a reviewer ladder: peer expert → PM → leaders → exec.

## Signals of success

The exec repeats your framing to others; the exec uses the product (wall of fame); corrections arrive as small monthly nudges, not as a "this is not what I want" after three months.
