# Guide — Interviewing senior engineers

**Load when:** preparing or running a hiring interview, or debriefing one. (For full interview prep, prefer the dedicated interview skills; this is the director's lens.)

## Default moves

1. **Frame:** intro → "why leave?" → "what's your dream company?" → agenda (deep dive ~20 min, co-design ~30 min, their tough questions 5–10 min).
2. **Psychological safety:** "I only add points. My goal is to find what you know, not what you don't. 'I don't know' is fine."
3. **Pick the project with the most ownership,** ideally from scratch, and define terms early.
4. **Probe the decision behind the numbers** ("why 1.25 and not 1.5?"), ask what broke, push to the limits with hints, and end on **the lesson learned**.
5. **Design challenge:** let them ask clarifying questions; push for alternatives; check where load is triaged (write vs read).
6. **Over-answering candidate:** "Simple question, simple answer; I'll drive."
7. **Gaps and job-hops:** ask respectfully, accept honest answers, then move on to motivation.
8. **Selling honestly:** scope grows with proven competence; occasional crunch, never a habit; ownership of a product, not disconnected projects.
9. **Protect the team's load:** cap interviews per person per week; new interviewers shadow first and debrief after.
