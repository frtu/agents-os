# Guide — Performance reviews, promotions and 1:1s

**Load when:** writing or delivering a mid-year or annual review, a promotion or comp talk, or structuring a coaching 1:1. Case: S. Template: `../templates/mid-year-review.md`.

## Default moves

1. **1:1 structure:** past / present / future. Report lists FYI items first, then the decisions needed. Time-box it. Unblock the present before discussing the future.
2. **Care first:** health, leave, load. Recommend the leave length that fits the upcoming crunch ("it's about planning").
3. **Continue / stop / start, framed as balance.** Use specific recent examples and an image that sticks.
4. **Recurring improvement themes:**
   - Decompose the **WHEN**, not only the HOW (H17).
   - Escalate earlier on hard problems.
   - Move from strict-everywhere to case-by-case judgement; a lower bar is safe only with a second gate.
   - Depth is proven, not told: demo every couple of weeks and make noise.
   - Influence ladder: "I tell you how" → "you tell me how" → "I share my problem".
   - From executor to guide: set the frame, let others build, course-correct.
5. **Reframe business-as-usual:** 40–50% is healthy because the product is used; only 70–80% is a problem.
6. **Own your share:** "partly my fault, I keep planting seeds."
7. **Promotion and comp:** tell them before the HR system does. Number first, then "what do you think?". Explain the context honestly. Admit what you don't know yet, commit to follow up, and point to the people partner.
8. **Ask for feedback** and act on flags about peers' load.

## Questions to fire

- "Which recent example proves this?"
- "Is this a WHEN problem or a HOW problem?"
- "What did this demand that the level below couldn't?" (H12)

## Traps

- Generic strengths ("good work").
- Turning a signature strength into a fault.
- Letting the HR system deliver the news.
- Opening new topics in the review ("no new topics").
