# Case M — Strategic vendor under cost pressure

- **Before:** *"We will negotiate a 15% price reduction and ask all teams to reduce usage."*
- **Common approach:** squeeze the price, cut usage everywhere.
- **Why this one diverges:** the goal is money-to-value, not lower spend. Cutting zero-value usage does not lower the vendor's perceived value. It buys room for high-value adoption.
- **Interrogation:** *"Which usage has zero value?"* *"Which cost lines does the vendor earn on, and which pass through?"* *"What high-value use would the savings fund?"*
- **What surfaces:** abandoned test environments, overlong retention, and a vendor happy to help on pass-through costs but nervous about its revenue lines.
- **Distilled (what to do):** drop the dead weight so the balloon climbs. P1 cut the superfluous; P2 move up the value chain, starting now; P3 new bets. Tell the vendor the mandate openly. Ask for specific outputs, not "think for me". Swap, don't add. Adoption: know → see value → see myself using it, through prototypes grounded in the team's use case.
- **Lesson:** cost pressure is the moment to move from quantity to quality of use.
