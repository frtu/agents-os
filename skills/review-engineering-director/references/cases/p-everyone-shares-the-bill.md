# Case P — One party lags and everyone shares the bill

- **Before:** a program update: *"To protect the date, all teams will pick up part of the remaining scope."*
- **Common approach:** spread the load to hold the date.
- **Why this one diverges:** the teams that always pay step back, and silent non-objection becomes unofficial pushback: people say yes and don't do it. It is the late party's manager's staffing problem.
- **Interrogation:** *"Whose scope is late, and what did their leader do about capacity?"* (19). *"What's the real headcount, not the claimed one?"* *"Is any vertical slice complete end to end?"*
- **What surfaces:** every layer touched, none finished. No exit gates. Open-forum meetings with no focus.
- **Distilled (what to do):** pre-align roles in a 1:1 or cancel the group meeting. One topic per meeting. Exit gates per step with lightweight checklists ("test your scope"). Vertical slices. Capacity rule: owner → owner's leader finds people → else shift the date; never melt in the middle.
- **Lesson:** fairness is a delivery tool. The reliable teams are the first to stop cooperating.
