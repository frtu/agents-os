# Case L — A new boss arrives

- **Before:** a first-1:1 deck: org chart, roadmap, a page of wins, *"all systems stable"*.
- **Common approach:** present the roadmap, or defend what exists.
- **Why this one diverges:** the new boss wants **pain points**, not perfection. Without proactive sharing he steers blind, and you spend months saying "that's not how it is".
- **Interrogation:** *"What hurts, for whom, how often, and is there a graph?"* *"Has value been established before cost or migration?"* (21).
- **What surfaces:** friction for each group you serve; one proposal of his with value in one part and none in another; your own team's overload.
- **Distilled (what to do):** ask for his top-down intuition first, then give the bottom-up view. Build a pain-point inventory by who you serve. Problems first. Order proposals value → cost → migration. Steer his idea toward the real value and give him a topic to drive. Describe the predecessor neutrally. Set a sync cadence.
- **Lesson:** proactive transparency upward prevents being steered blind.
