# Template — Vision refresh for an exec

Use after an exec judged the strategy from an increment ("wrong direction"), or when the vision hasn't been presented to an exec for more than ~3 months. Pairs with Case E and `../guides/exec-review-and-demo.md`.

```markdown
# [Platform/area]: where we're going and how it connects

**Thesis (one line):** [e.g. "An agent is only as good as the context it can reach."]

## The end shape  (logical architecture, one diagram)
[Boxes = capabilities; arrows = how they connect to company products and partners.
 Solid line = shipped; dotted = in progress; grey = later.]

## Why these pieces belong together
[2–3 bullets: customer/SLA reasons, reuse, security.]

## Where we are on the path
| Milestone | Capability unlocked | Status | Who uses it |
|---|---|---|---|

## What it can't do yet — and why
[Honest limits + what it would take.]

## What we need from you
[Priority call, sponsorship for a partner, budget, a decision.]
```

**Section insight**
- Show the end shape first. The milestone table exists to prove momentum, not to define the vision.
- Use the exec's vocabulary for capabilities, and avoid labels that bias ("don't call it a training platform").
- Your boss reviews it first, then you present together.
