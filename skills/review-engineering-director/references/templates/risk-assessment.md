# Template — Risk assessment ("should we go?")

Use when leaders ask for a risk evaluation of a migration, launch or cross-company change. Pairs with Case R.

```markdown
# [Change]: risk assessment

**Bottom line:** [Go / no-go / go-with-conditions + the single biggest risk.]

## What leadership fears most
[e.g. an irreversible half-done migration; customer-visible downtime.]

## Risks by theme
### [Theme 1 — e.g. data consistency]
- Risk: [technical fact] → **Business consequence:** [plain language] → Sample case: [one concrete example]
- Mitigation / owner to answer: [domain/team]
### [Theme 2 — blast radius]
### [Theme 3 — detection & recovery (MTTD / MTTR)]

## Undefined terms that change the answer
[e.g. "zero downtime": read vs write?]

## Conditions to go
[Checklist that turns no-go into go.]
```

**Section insight**
- No action plan. Leaders want to know what happens if it goes wrong.
- Group by theme, with each risk next to its mitigation, not two separate lists.
- Let owners answer their risks; you are the consulting expert, not the program manager.
