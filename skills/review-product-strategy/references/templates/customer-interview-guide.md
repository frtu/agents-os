# Template — Customer discovery interview (30 min)

Use to gather pain points before a strategy or pitch, or to audit how interviews were run. Pairs with Case H and `../guides/pain-points-and-evidence.md`.

```markdown
# Interview — [role, team/company] — [YYYY-MM-DD]   Payer | User

| Block | Time | Prompts |
|-------|------|---------|
| Context | 3' | Role, what they own, how success is measured |
| Last time | 10' | "Tell me about the last time you hit [problem]." Step by step; who was involved |
| Cost | 7' | How often? How long? What did it cost — hours, money, incidents, customers? |
| Workaround | 5' | What do you do today? What did you try or buy? Why didn't it stick? |
| Priority | 3' | Rank vs other problems; who else feels it; who pays |
| Close | 2' | Quote permission · who else to talk to · pilot / feedback commitment |

## Notes
- Story (verbatim):
- Frequency / time / cost:
- Workaround:
- Quote (permission Y/N): ""
- Commitment offered: intro | pilot | budget | none
- Signal: strong | weak — why
```

**Section insight**
- No solution talk before Close; past tense, not "would you…".
- They should talk more than you; note their vocabulary — it belongs in the customer pitch.
