# Template — Pain-point evidence log

Use to collect and audit the evidence behind a problem statement, strategy or pitch. Pairs with Cases C, H and `../guides/pain-points-and-evidence.md`. Every problem / traction claim should trace to a row.

```markdown
# Evidence log — [product / initiative]

## Pain points
| # | Who (segment, payer/user) | "Last time…" story | Frequency | Cost (time / $ / incidents) | Workaround | Source | Strength |
|---|---------------------------|--------------------|-----------|-----------------------------|------------|--------|----------|

## Testimonials
| Verbatim quote | Role | Permission to name | Date | Use on |
|----------------|------|--------------------|------|--------|

## Metrics
| Metric | Exact definition | Now | Trend (4–6 periods) | Source |
|--------|------------------|-----|---------------------|--------|

## Hero facts (2–4)
1. [number] — proves [claim]

## Objections / Q&A bank
| Question | Asked by | Answer | Data | Appendix slide |
|----------|----------|--------|------|----------------|
```

**Section insight**
- Strength = strong only for past behaviour, workaround, spend or commitment.
- Existing signals count: tickets, postmortems, escalations, churn notes — cite them.
- An empty pain-point table means the strategy's "Problem" dimension is unproven, whatever the prose says.
