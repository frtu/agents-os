# Template — Pitch deck / product presentation

Use when preparing a funding ask, product presentation or internal initiative pitch, or as a checklist in Pitch review. Pairs with Cases A, B, D, F, I and `../guides/pitch-narrative-and-structure.md`. Never fill a slide with invented data — mark gaps `[need evidence]`.

```markdown
# [Company / initiative] — pitch to [audience]

**Pitch type:** investor/exec | customer/adopting team   **Ask:** [amount / headcount / mandate]
**Decision wanted:** [...]   **Slot:** [N] min + [M] min Q&A

## Spine (write first — one claim per slide)
| # | Claim (= slide title) | Evidence (source) | Phase |
|---|-----------------------|-------------------|-------|
| 1 | | | Attention / Trust / Conviction / Action |

## Slides
1. Title — one-liner (concrete, layperson, specific)
2. Traction teaser — one hero fact
3. Problem — today's workflow for the payer: X steps, Y time, Z% / cost
4. Solution — side-by-side with the problem, same units
5–7. Traction — revenue|adoption + model · acquisition + unit economics · retention / engagement
8. Insight / moat — what we know that others don't; why it's hard to copy; why now
9. Market — prospective customers × value per customer
10. Competition — honest positioning incl. status quo / buy-vs-build
11. Team — why us; lean / capital-efficient proof
12. Vision — logical extension of the traction
13. Ask — amount, use, milestone in [18–24 months | next quarters]
A. Appendix — one slide per anticipated question; projections; detailed use of funds
```

**Section insight**
- Build order ≠ show order: problem, team, market first; title slide last.
- Every slide passes the flash test; topic titles ("Metrics") are a smell — titles are claims.
- Internal: traction = adoption / toil / incidents; model = cost / funding; competition = status quo.
