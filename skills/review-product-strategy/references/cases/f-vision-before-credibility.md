# Case F — Vision before credibility

- **Before:** slide 2 is "Our vision: the operating system for X". Traction is on slide 11.
- **Common approach:** make the vision bolder to compensate for thin traction.
- **Why this one diverges:** an unearned vision is hand-waving; a bolder one is louder hand-waving. At later stages the audience wants to know what you have to show for the time and money already spent.
- **Interrogation:** *"What have you already built, and who uses it?"* (P8). *"Is the vision a logical extension of that traction?"* *"Is the obstacle to growth really resources — or is it product-market fit?"*
- **What surfaces:** the vision is fine; the sequencing makes it unbelievable. Sometimes it reveals the opposite — there is no traction yet, so the pitch should be seed-style (team, insight) rather than pretending.
- **Distilled (what to do):** traction teaser early to earn attention; problem → solution → in-depth proof; *then* "how this becomes a very large business". End by showing the only missing input is attention and resources, and how the ask converts into the next milestone.
- **Lesson:** earn the right to dream. Internally, the same rule: answer "be ambitious" with an adopted increment, not a bigger promise.
