# Case E — Number dump

- **Before:** twelve KPIs on one slide; a "total users since launch" curve; a two-axis chart; "revenue" with no gross/net.
- **Common approach:** add more data to look rigorous — or remove numbers that look weak.
- **Why this one diverges:** both moves damage trust. Too many numbers means nothing is retained; too few looks like hiding; **cumulative charts** almost always hide weak recent periods and an expert reader knows it.
- **Interrogation:** *"Which 2–4 numbers do you want repeated when they pitch you to their partners?"* (P6). *"Monthly or daily actives? Gross or net?"* *"What does the last 4–6 months look like, per period?"* *"Which line goes with which axis?"* (P10).
- **What surfaces:** the most important metric for *this* audience isn't the one on top (e.g. signups first, while revenue or retention is the real story).
- **Distilled (what to do):** lead with the metric this audience cares about and explain its driver (business model). Choose hero facts. Show per-period trends with the conclusion written as the chart caption. Define every metric. Move the rest to the appendix.
- **Lesson:** numbers persuade only when the listener understands them at a glance — and believes you computed them honestly.
