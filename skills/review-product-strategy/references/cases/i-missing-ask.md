# Case I — The missing ask

- **Before:** last slide "Join us on the journey". Or "We need 10 engineers" with no milestone attached.
- **Common approach:** add a number at the end.
- **Why this one diverges:** the ask is the climax the whole deck builds to; bolted on, it's unconvincing. And if the real blocker is product-market fit or execution, asking for resources invites the wrong question.
- **Interrogation:** *"How much, for what, reaching which milestone by when (18–24 months; internally the next 2–4 quarters)?"* (P11). *"Why is attention and resources the obstacle — not the product?"* (P8). *"What happens if the answer is no?"*
- **What surfaces:** the milestone the ask buys is often unclear even to the team; sometimes the honest ask is smaller (a pilot, a sponsor, a decision) rather than money.
- **Distilled (what to do):** state amount, high-level use, and the traction milestone it reaches; tie it back to the evidence. Internally: headcount / budget / mandate + the milestone it buys + what stops if not funded. Make "yes" cheap: offer the appendix or data room proactively.
- **Lesson:** no ask, no decision.
